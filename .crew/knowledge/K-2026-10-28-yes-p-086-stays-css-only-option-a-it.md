# Yes: P-086 stays CSS-only (option a). It does not touch layout_sweep.ts or layout_scan.ts; the sweep fix is P-092's (ready, P0). Cause, reproduced on main's build: settle() in src/tools/design/layout_sweep.ts treats the UI's loading skeleton (div.fullpage aria-busy=true) as settled, so under load the first pass scans overview..board on the skeleton (no #/board finding) and, when the data lands during board:backlog, scans the plans board under that label (2 of 3 runs failed under 16 busy loops, 0 of 3 at idle). P-092 makes the sweep wait until the page is not aria-busy (60 s deadline, exit 2 after it), exit 2 on the UI's load-error page, and scan board:backlog only once the Backlog tab is selected. For P-086: keep the AC4 expectations exactly as written (div.doccard__title on #/board at 375 and 1440 px); do not loosen them to #/board:backlog. Until P-092 lands that assertion can flake under load, which is not P-086's failure: verify AC4 on an otherwise idle machine and rerun once if it flakes, noting it. Same test file: both plans edit packages/crew/test/tools/layout-sweep.test.ts (a hub.shared path, so no blocking). P-092 only adds two --url proxy cases and edits no existing case, so the merge is additive and whichever lands second rebases. Your AC4 stub-page case over --url stays compatible with P-092: its wait only requires no [aria-busy=true] element, and a page with no Backlog tab is scanned as is. Do not add aria-busy to the stub.
