# Mostly right, with one change: drop the 'if scope-related' part. The hub cannot tell whether an escalation is about scope; it only sees the ref, so the rule has to depend on the ref alone. Rule: an open escalation about a goal holds that goal only. The goal's forward moves are refused, because the blocking question already does that, and dispatch gives no entry whose ref is the goal (for example 'PM closes G-###'). Its plans and items keep moving. If the asker needs work under the goal to wait as well, it escalates on those plans or items, or the Director pauses the team. An escalation stops the team only when it has no ref or its ref is the IDEA. There is no policy ref kind (kindOf knows idea/goal/plan/item), so a policy escalation is a no-ref one. The 'escalations hold every open plan and item' stop stays as it is, and held goals don't count toward it. If the focus goal is held and nothing else can move, dispatch's stall entry reports it. WHERE: one place in code, escalationHolds() in packages/crew/src/hub/commands/team/questions.ts. Change the test to kind === 'plan' || 'item' || 'goal', and fix its doc comment. run --check (autonomy.ts runState), dispatch.ts and inbox.ts all use it, so inbox shows 'holds G-005' with no change of its own. Update the docstring in autonomy.ts runState. Docs: plugins/crew/skills/hub/references/model.md (the Inbox paragraph: 'holds P-###' for a plan, item or goal, else 'stops the team' for no ref or the idea; the Dispatch paragraph's 'Documents an escalation holds'), plugins/crew/skills/team/SKILL.md (the 'It stops' line: 'an escalation with no plan, item or goal to hold'), and the commands.md entry if it repeats the rule. Targeted tests: packages/crew/test/hub/run-check.test.ts (a goal escalation gives HELD G-### and exit 0; a no-ref escalation and an IDEA escalation still give STOP) and packages/crew/test/hub/e2e/inbox.test.ts (holds G-###).
