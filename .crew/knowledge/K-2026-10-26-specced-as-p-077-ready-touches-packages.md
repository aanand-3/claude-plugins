# Specced as P-077 (ready; touches packages/crew/test/setup/sample.ts (new), test/view.test.ts, test/mcp.test.ts; no dependencies). BUG-028's fix (P-043: one full suite at a time via crew.mjs suite, maxWorkers 50%) still holds, since only one suite ran in each bounce. It is no longer enough because the load now comes from outside the suite: parallel developer/QA agents' targeted vitest runs, builds and sessions, which no slot covers, plus a suite that grew from 371 to 654 tests. The breaking part is the fixed 120 s beforeAll limit on a deterministic setup (about 36 sequential bundle spawns) whose wall time follows load. View's hook is synchronous, so the limit never caught a hang; it only failed setups that finished slowly. Chosen fix: a shared test helper buildSample() gives the sample-project child a 600 s execFileSync timeout (the real hang deadline), and both sample-project beforeAlls use a 660 s hook timeout. Rejected: serialising the suite further (slower landings, does nothing about outside load), load-scaled limits (load changes during the run), building once in globalSetup (adds the build to every targeted run). CHORE-025 stays the structural fix. Regression: AC4 runs view.test.ts under 16 busy-loop processes, passing on the branch and failing on the base branch.

Answer to Q-a873f3 (architect): Spec a plan for BUG-049 (G-007, P1): full-suite view.test.ts beforeAll (execFileSync sample-project.mjs + export) times out under load; it bounced the landing of P-062 and P-064 (full suite 1327 s vs usual 620-940 s; the test passes alone in 73 s). It is BUG-028 recurring, so first check what BUG-028's fix did and why it no longer holds. Options: a bigger or load-scaled beforeAll timeout, cheaper setup (build the sample project once, or share it), or serialising the full suite. Please spec it as a ready plan so Release can land P-062 and P-064 reliably.
