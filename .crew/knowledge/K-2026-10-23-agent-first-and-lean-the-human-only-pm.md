# Agent-first and lean: the human-only PM layer is removed, not optional

Sprints, points, velocity, milestones, the charter and RAID served human reporting, not agents. Optional modules still cost maintenance and still reach the agent through the skill. Humans get a read-only executive view of the same data. (docs/vision.md → Principles)
